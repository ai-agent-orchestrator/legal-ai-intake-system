Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished high-fidelity desktop web application login mockup image
for "Legal Intake" / Security-Aware Legal AI Intake System.
This is Screen 01, a NEW COMPARISON CANDIDATE, not a replacement of the existing login.
Render the actual application viewport, approximately 1440 x 1024, straight-on,
with no device frame, browser chrome, perspective, watermark, or explanatory callouts.
All product UI text must be legible Korean, except the English product name.

MOOD AND VISUAL SYSTEM
Professional, trustworthy, calm, practical legal-tech SaaS.
Background #F6F7F9; surface #FFFFFF; primary text #182230; muted #667085;
borders #D0D5DD; primary blue #175CD3; soft blue #EFF8FF.
Readable Korean sans-serif typography such as Pretendard or Noto Sans KR.
Use a generous whitespace rhythm, thin borders, subtle shadows, modest 8-12px
corner radii, and a restrained blue accent. The page should feel implementable in React.

COMPOSITION
A minimal top application header with a small blue geometric mark and
the product name "Legal Intake" at the left.
A balanced centered white login panel, approximately 520px wide, with generous
inner padding and a clean single-column reading order.
Do not turn the viewport into a promotional hero or add decorative legal imagery.
